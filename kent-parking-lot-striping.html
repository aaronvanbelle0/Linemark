What Kent Lots Are Up Against

Heavy Truck Traffic

Loaded trailers turning and backing into docks grind paint off faster than car traffic. Dock approaches and truck lanes usually wear out first.

Operations That Never Stop

Many Kent facilities run multiple shifts. Striping has to fit around shift changes and deliveries without blocking trucks.

Wet, Flat Valley Pavement

Flat valley lots can hold water after storms, and paint won't bond to wet pavement. We schedule around dry windows so the lines last.

Kent Striping FAQs

Can you stripe a Kent warehouse lot without stopping trucks?

Yes. We can work one section of the truck court or parking area at a time and schedule around your shifts and delivery windows, so trucks keep moving while we stripe.

Do you mark warehouse floors as well as outside lots?

Yes. Along with truck courts and parking lots, we mark forklift lanes, pedestrian walkways, staging areas, and safety zones inside warehouses and distribution centers.

How often should truck courts be restriped?

Most commercial lots need restriping every 2 to 3 years, but truck courts, dock approaches, and forklift routes with heavy daily traffic often need it sooner.
