Port and logistics traffic grinds down dock approaches and truck lanes quickly. Those areas usually need restriping before the rest of the lot.

Big Retail Lots

Large shopping center lots have long runs of stalls and drive aisles. Clear arrows and crosswalks keep traffic flowing and pedestrians safe.

Wet Season Timing

Paint needs dry pavement and temperatures above roughly 50°F to cure properly, so most exterior striping in Tacoma happens from late spring to early fall.
