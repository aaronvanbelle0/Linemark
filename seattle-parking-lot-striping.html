Parking Lot Striping Built for Seattle Conditions

Seattle lots work hard. Months of rain keep pavement damp, moss creeps over faded lines in shaded corners, and worn markings are hardest to read on wet, dark asphalt, exactly when drivers need them most. LineMark lays down crisp, bright lines with undiluted Sherwin-Williams PROMAR traffic paint on a clean, dry surface so they hold up to Seattle traffic and weather.

Warehouse and Truck Court Marking in SoDo, Georgetown and the Kent Valley

Distribution and industrial properties along the Duwamish see constant truck and forklift traffic. We mark forklift aisles, pedestrian walkways, loading dock zones and truck court layouts so drivers and crews know where to be. See our warehouse and factory markings.

ADA and Fire Lane Compliance for Seattle Medical and Multifamily Properties

Faded accessible stalls, access aisles and fire lanes aren't cosmetic. Missing or unreadable markings can leave a property out of ADA and fire code compliance. We restore ADA-compliant stalls and fire lane markings for clinics on First Hill and apartment communities across the city.

Night and Weekend Striping Across King County

Seattle lots are small and busy, and most can't close during the day. We schedule early mornings, nights and Saturdays so retail centers, medical offices and warehouses stay open while the work gets done.

How a Seattle Striping Job Works

  1. Quote. Send photos or an address, or we walk the lot. You get a written quote noting any layout changes, ADA updates or added stencils.
  2. Schedule. We book a dry-weather window, early morning, night or Saturday if your business needs to stay open.
  3. Prep. The lot is cleared of vehicles with about 3 feet around every line. We can quote sweeping or pressure washing if it needs it.
  4. Stripe and reopen. We repaint the lines and keep traffic off until they're dry, typically 30 to 60 minutes in good weather.

FAQ

Seattle Striping Questions

What parts of Seattle and King County does LineMark serve?

We stripe lots across Seattle, including SoDo, Georgetown, Ballard, Interbay, Northgate, Lake City, First Hill, and the Rainier Valley, plus Shoreline, Bellevue, Kirkland, Redmond, Renton, Tukwila, Kent, Auburn, Federal Way, and Tacoma.

How do you schedule parking lot striping around Seattle's rain?

Traffic paint needs dry pavement to bond. We watch the forecast closely for every Seattle job and book around dry windows, with most striping done between late spring and early fall. If rain moves in, we reschedule rather than put down paint that won't last.

Can you stripe at night so my Seattle business doesn't have to close?

Yes. We offer night, early-morning, and weekend striping for Seattle retail centers, warehouses, and medical offices so your lot is marked and dry before customers, trucks, or patients arrive.

Can you mark warehouse floors and truck courts in SoDo and Georgetown?

Yes. We handle forklift lanes, pedestrian walkways, loading dock markings, and truck court striping for industrial and distribution properties in SoDo, Georgetown, along the Duwamish, and in the Kent Valley.

How long until cars can park on new lines?

In good weather, lines are typically dry in 30 to 60 minutes. We keep traffic off fresh paint until it's ready, then reopen the lot.

How often should a Seattle parking lot be restriped?

Most commercial lots need restriping every 2 to 3 years. Busy entrances, delivery areas, and shaded, damp spots where moss grows can wear faster.

What paint do you use?

We use undiluted Sherwin-Williams PROMAR traffic marking paint for our parking lot line work, applied to a clean, dry surface for the best bond.
