What Renton Lots Are Up Against

Mixed Traffic

Delivery trucks, shoppers, patients, and residents all use Renton lots differently. Clear arrows, stop bars, and crosswalks keep them from getting in each other's way.

Fire Access at Apartments

Apartment complexes and HOAs need fire lanes that stay readable. Faded red curbs and stencils make it easy for residents to block emergency access.

Aging Asphalt

Older lots develop cracks and gray, oxidized pavement that makes new lines look patchy. Sealcoating before restriping gives lines a clean, dark background.

Renton Striping FAQs

Can you number stalls and paint reserved or guest parking for Renton apartments?

Yes. We paint stall numbers and reserved, guest, and visitor parking stencils for apartment communities and HOAs, along with full restriping and fire lanes.

Do you repaint fire lanes and red curbs?

Yes. We repaint red curbs and FIRE LANE stencils so emergency access stays clearly marked. Your local fire marshal sets the exact requirements, so we match the markings to what your property needs to show.

Can you sealcoat a Renton lot before restriping?

Yes. Sealcoating protects the asphalt and gives new lines a clean, dark background. Most sealcoating needs 24 to 48 hours of cure time before vehicle traffic, and then we stripe.
