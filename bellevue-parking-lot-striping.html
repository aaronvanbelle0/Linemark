Bellevue Striping FAQs

Can you restripe a Bellevue office park without shutting it down?

Yes. We can stripe a large lot one section at a time, or work early mornings and after hours, so tenants and visitors always have somewhere to park. In good weather, lines are typically dry in 30 to 60 minutes.

How many ADA parking spaces does my Bellevue lot need?

Under the 2010 ADA Standards, a lot with 1 to 25 spaces needs 1 accessible space, 26 to 50 needs 2, 51 to 75 needs 3, and 76 to 100 needs 4. At least one of every six accessible spaces must be van accessible, and medical facilities need more. We can check your count when we quote the job.

How often should a Bellevue parking lot be restriped?

Most commercial lots need restriping every 2 to 3 years. Busy entrances, drive-throughs, and shaded, damp areas can wear faster.
